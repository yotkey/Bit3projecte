Who We Are

We are Adrián Hernández, Álvaro González, and Miguel Peiró, three students who have brought our ideas and skills together to bring Bit3 to life. This project was born as part of the Project subject, where we have set out to develop a prototype from scratch, going through the entire process: from the initial idea to the final result.

Throughout the course, we will document every step of our work on this blog, sharing both our progress and the obstacles we encounter along the way. We want this space to reflect not only what we build, but also how we learn to plan, design, and work as a team.

Third Week of the Project

During this third week, we focused on laying the foundations in Arduino to begin developing the project. We started experimenting with the components and basic programming needed to make our initial ideas work.

We spent the week testing different Arduino functions and understanding how the different components would interact with each other. This allowed us to identify the main technical challenges we will have to address as the project progresses.

We finished the week with a better understanding of the Arduino environment and the basic elements needed to continue developing our project. This gave us a solid starting point for the next stages of Bit3.

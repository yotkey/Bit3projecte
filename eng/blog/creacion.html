The Origin of Bit3: How This Project Began

Bit3 started as a simple idea between three classmates who wanted to create something of our own. During the first sessions of the Project subject, we realized that we shared the same interest: not simply following instructions, but designing and building a solution from scratch, learning at every step of the process.

The name "Bit3" came from thinking about technology (the bit, the smallest unit of information) and the fact that there are three of us on the team. We thought it was a simple name, easy to remember, and one that represented what we wanted to convey: a small project in its early stages, but with the intention of growing and providing something useful.

That is how this blog was born, as a space to share the evolution of the project week by week, including both our achievements and the difficulties we encounter along the way.

Second Week of the Project

During this second week, we focused on laying the foundations of the project. We started by sharing our ideas and discussing which direction we wanted to take Bit3 in, until we clearly defined what we wanted to achieve and why.

We also introduced the project to the class, presenting our initial proposal and receiving the first impressions from our classmates and teachers. It was a good starting point for identifying which aspects we needed to refine before moving forward.

We ended the week by making the initial distribution of tasks among the three team members, according to each person's strengths, and with the website already up and running so we could start documenting the entire process from the very beginning.
